Capabilities

How aiRA keeps customers from slipping away

Early warning, per customer

aiRA measures each customer against their own normal, not a global average — so a weekly shopper who slows down gets flagged long before a monthly one looks unusual.

The fix matched to the reason

Price-sensitive, let down by service, or simply drifting — aiRA matches the response to the likely cause, because one win-back offer does not fit all.

Works inside your loyalty program

Saves happen through your own program — bonus points, tier protection, reward reminders — always within its rules and budgets.

Win-back that doesn't give up on schedule

Lapsed customers stay covered by agents that keep testing timing, channel, and offer — and know when your rules say stop.
