Capabilities

How aiRA works every visit

Reads each visitor's moment

aiRA combines what a visitor is doing right now with everything your systems already know about them — loyalty status, past purchases, service history — into one live picture.

The right next step, including none

For each visitor, aiRA chooses among an offer, a message, a nudge — or nothing at all. The wrong discount costs margin. The right silence builds trust.

Works through your existing channels

Decisions go out through the tools you already run — on-site messages, email, app, or a handoff to your sales team. No new software for customers to see or staff to learn.

Protects your margins

You set the limits: maximum discounts, how often a customer can receive an offer, and who is excluded. Agents work hard inside those limits — and never outside them.
