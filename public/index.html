Questions

What executives ask us

aiRA is an AI agent that drives growth across your business. It connects to your systems of record — loyalty engine, CDP, campaign manager, commerce — finds revenue opportunities in your customer base, plans the campaigns to capture them, and executes. Consequential actions wait for your approval.

You define the rules: discount caps, audience thresholds, spend limits, compliance gates. aiRA executes freely inside those rules and stops at the line. Anything that crosses a threshold is held with the reason stated, until a person approves it. Every action — automatic or approved — lands in an audit trail.

Automation platforms run the journeys you build. aiRA does the work before and after: it finds the signal, sizes the opportunity, designs the plan, configures it in your existing stack, and measures what happened. Your automation platform is one of the systems it operates — not the thing it replaces.

A chatbot answers questions; the work still lands on your team. aiRA is grounded in your customer data and connected to your execution systems, so a plan doesn't end as a suggestion — it ends as a configured campaign, a redesigned programme, or an offer live in your engine. With a record of every step, and a human sign-off where it matters.

Your systems of record: typically the loyalty engine, CDP or data warehouse, campaign manager, and offer engine. Access is scoped per system and per action — read where it analyzes, write only where you've allowed it to execute. It starts narrow and widens only as your team grants it.

Customer data stays in your systems; aiRA operates on it where it lives. Every action is permissioned, logged, and reversible through the audit trail. Regulated workflows — banking, healthcare — route through your compliance process before anything reaches a customer. Governance is the product's spine, not a setting.
