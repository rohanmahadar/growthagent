What is aiRA

A System of Intelligence, above your Systems of Record

Your business runs on Systems of Record — the CRM, billing, loyalty, and data tools that store what happened. They are excellent at keeping records. They cannot decide what to do next. aiRA is the layer that sits above them: it reads what they know, decides what should happen next, and acts — inside the tools you already own.

Questions

What enterprise teams ask us first

How does aiRA connect to the systems we already run?

Through standard, secure connections to the tools most enterprises use — data platforms like Snowflake and BigQuery, CRMs like Salesforce and Microsoft Dynamics, plus billing, loyalty, and commerce systems. Anything custom connects through standard APIs, the same way your systems already talk to each other. aiRA reads from your systems and writes actions back into them. It replaces nothing.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Your data is used only to inform aiRA's decisions for your business. It is not used to train models shared with other customers. Data handling, storage, and location are covered in detail during the demo and security review.

How are the agents' actions controlled?

You set the rules: what aiRA may do on its own, what needs a person's approval, spending limits, and customer privacy rules. Agents work freely inside those rules and stop at them. Every action is recorded together with the reasoning behind it, so your risk and compliance teams can audit everything, end to end.

How quickly do we see value?

Most teams start with one goal and a few connected systems, and see agents proposing actions — for approval — within the first weeks of a pilot. Not after a multi-quarter project. Your demo includes a pilot plan scoped to your setup.

How is this different from a copilot or a dashboard?

Dashboards tell you what happened. Copilots help one person work faster inside one tool. aiRA is different: it reads across all your systems, decides what should happen next, and carries it out through always-on agents — while your team approves and oversees, instead of doing the manual work.

Which systems does aiRA work with?

Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Salesforce, SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Segment, Adobe, Shopify, and more — plus standard APIs and webhooks for the rest. If a system stores records or carries out actions, aiRA can likely work with it. We confirm your exact setup during the demo.
