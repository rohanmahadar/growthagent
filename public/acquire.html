Capabilities

How aiRA finds your next best customers

Audiences built from your best customers

aiRA builds target audiences from your actual highest-value customers, not generic demographic templates. You attract people who look like your best — not just your most numerous.

Always-on prospecting agents

Agents test audiences, messages, and channels continuously, moving budget toward what converts. Smaller niche audiences get worked too, not skipped.

Built on your own data

Every audience decision is based on your own customer records — always respecting each person's privacy choices, and traceable to the signals behind it.

Qualified handoff into your systems

New prospects don't land in a spreadsheet. aiRA scores them and adds them to your CRM and marketing tools with their history attached — ready for the next step.
