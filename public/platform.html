One picture, no new copy of your data

aiRA reads customers, transactions, stock, and campaign history where they already live. Your data stays put, under the controls you already have.

Live, not last-quarter

The picture refreshes continuously. Agents decide on today's signals, not last month's report.

Speaks your language

aiRA understands your business terms — tiers, products, regions, campaigns — so plans come back in plain English, not database names.

Evidence attached

Every recommendation shows the records and signals behind it. Your team can check the work before anyone approves it.

Decide → Act

Intelligence that ends in your systems, not in a slide deck

Most AI stops at an insight. aiRA finishes the job: decisions become actions, and actions land back inside your systems — where they are carried out, measured, and recorded.

Plans, not just answers

Ask a question, get a plan: which customers, what actions, in what order, and the expected result — ready for your approval, not your interpretation.

Work done in your tools

Agents write the work back: campaigns into your marketing platform, offers into your loyalty program, updated lists into your customer database. Live in the tools your team checks daily.

People pointed to the right place

When a step needs a person, aiRA sends them to the exact system — or the right website — with the background already attached, ready to act.

Results measured at the source

Outcomes are read back from the same systems the actions ran in — closing the loop between decision, action, and result.

Governance

You set the rules. aiRA stays inside them.

Autonomy without accountability is a liability. aiRA is built so your risk, compliance, and security teams can say yes — and keep saying yes.

Approval gates

You decide which actions run on their own and which pause for a person. These are enforced controls, not promises.

Full audit trail

Goal, evidence, decision, action, result — every step recorded and time-stamped, end to end.

Privacy & consent built in

Each customer's privacy choices, as recorded in your systems, are honored by every agent, on every channel.
