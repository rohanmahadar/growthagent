How it works

Signal to sign-off, step by step

The same four-step loop runs everything aiRA does — signal, plan, ask, execute. Here it is, with nothing hidden.

01Signal

A movement in your data starts the loop

aiRA reads your systems of record continuously — loyalty engine, CDP, commerce, campaign manager. When a metric drifts from its baseline, the drift becomes a signal. Sized, sourced, timestamped.

What your team seesOne card: the metric, the delta, and every system the signal was read from.

01 · signal feed · fictional
TUE 09:12 · SIGNAL DETECTED
Repeat purchases are slipping in the North region
Repeat rate 41.8% → 36.2%−5.6pp over six weeks

The dip concentrates in two store clusters and one loyalty tier — 38,400 members.

Loyalty engine CDP Commerce & POS Data warehouse
02Plan

The signal becomes a plan you can read

aiRA drafts the response: who to reach, what each cohort gets, what to expect. Every line carries its own projection. No black box — the plan reads like a brief, not a log file.

What your team seesThe full draft — each play, its audience, its offer, and the effect it expects.

02 · plan draft · fictional
Retain agent Win-back offer for the 38,400 slipping members, split by tier expect +2.1pp repeat
Convert agent Bonus-points offer for the two affected store clusters expect +1.4pp repeat
Retain agent Hold-out group set aside so the lift can be measured clean baseline
Your rule — offers above a 12% discount wait for approval
03Ask

Your thresholds decide what ships and what waits

The thresholds are yours to set — discount caps, audience limits, spend ceilings, compliance routing. Inside them, aiRA executes freely. Anything that crosses one waits, with the threshold named and the reason stated, until a person decides.

What your team seesThe approval queue — routine actions recorded, the exception held with its reason.

03 · approval ledger · fictional
Segment rebuild — five lifecycle cohorts refreshed Recorded
Offer copy variants drafted for review Recorded
16% discount proposed for the slipping tier Crosses your 12% discount cap — held until you decide Waiting for you
Send-window check — inside the audience limit you set Recorded
04Execute

The approved plan runs in your systems

After sign-off, aiRA configures the work in the stack you already run — offer engine, campaign manager, messaging channels — and pushes it live. Then it measures the result against its own projection and reports back.

What your team seesThe outcome, read against a hold-out: what ran, what moved, what it was worth.

04 · outcome report · fictional
Weeks 1–6 · after your sign-off

Hold-out group — repeat rate36.4%
  • 2 offers configured and pushed to the offer engine
  • Every send logged against the plan your team approved

+4.5pp repeat rate over the hold-out — the slip recovered in six weeks.

What gets recorded

Every action, on the record

Every action lands in the audit trail — automatic or approved. Who set it in motion, what it touched, when it ran, and the rule it ran under. Nothing executes off the books.

Exceptions carry more: the threshold they crossed, the reason they were held, and the decision a person made. Held work stays held until someone says otherwise.
