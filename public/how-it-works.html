How it works

From one sentence to a system that never stops working

This is the full tour — every step between a goal typed in plain language and approved actions running inside the systems your business already uses. What you see below is what your team sees in the product.

Book a demo Start the tour
1 2 3 4 5
1

Step 1

State the goal in plain language

There is no query builder and no request form. You describe the business outcome you want, the way you'd brief a trusted lead. aiRA asks clarifying questions only when they change the plan — which customers, what timeframe, what limits — and confirms the brief back to you before doing anything.

2

Step 2

aiRA gathers the facts from your systems

Before proposing anything, aiRA does the homework. It looks across your connected systems — customer records, loyalty accounts, order history, email engagement — and shows you exactly what it found: the customer groups, the warning signs, and the size of the opportunity. Every number can be traced back to the system it came from.

3

Step 3

aiRA proposes the agent team

aiRA breaks the goal into distinct pieces of work and proposes a specialized agent for each — with its job, approach, and limits spelled out. Nothing runs yet. This is your review screen: adjust an agent's limits, remove one, or approve the team as proposed.

4

Step 4

Approved agents act — in your systems

On approval, agents do the work inside the tools your teams already run: campaigns go live in your marketing platform, offers are issued in your loyalty program, customer lists are updated in your customer database. When a step needs a person, aiRA points them to the right system with the background attached. Everything lands where your team can see it, run it, and check it.

5

Step 5

Agents improve, report, and retire

Agents measure results where they happen — in the same systems the actions ran in — then adjust, reallocate, and retire on their own, inside your rules. You get a running report of what changed, what worked, and what each agent decided to do about it. The system keeps getting better; your team stays in charge.
