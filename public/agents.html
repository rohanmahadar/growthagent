Acquire — find the growth

Find the customers you're not reaching

Acquire maps your base into lifecycle cohorts and scores each customer's propensity to transact. The gaps get names — untouched actives, reachable hibernators, regions your calendar misses. Then it builds the plays to close them, sized before you spend.

  • //01Maps cohorts your campaigns never touch
  • //02Scores transaction propensity, customer by customer
  • //03Proposes sized acquisition plays for approval

A Middle East value-fashion retailer narrowed a −9.6pp regional growth gap to −2.2pp in five weeks — and grew new customers 29.4% year on year.

Retain — keep the base

Keep the customers you fought for

Retain models your loyalty economics — earn rates, tiers, milestones, points liability. Every proposed change carries a revenue projection, so you see the math before the mechanics move. Discount discipline is a rule it works inside, not a hope.

  • //01Models points economics, tiers and liability
  • //02Simulates milestone rewards with revenue projections
  • //03Proposes the redesign — your caps enforced

A premium menswear retailer redesigned its loyalty programme end-to-end in 38 days, holding discount discipline at 10% or under.

How they work together

Different jobs. One operating loop.

The loop is the same for all three. It never skips the ask.

Signal— Plan— Ask— Execute
01 · Signal

Any agent can raise one — a slipping cohort, a stalled offer, an ageing programme.

02 · Plan

aiRA drafts the play from your data, with audience, channels and revenue math attached.

03 · Ask

Every consequential step stops here and waits for a named human to approve.

04 · Execute

Approved work is pushed to your systems of record, and every action is logged.
