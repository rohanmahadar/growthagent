Agents

One goal in. Always-on agents out.

Give aiRA a business objective and it assembles the team: AI agents, each with one clear job, each working within rules you set — around the clock, inside your systems. Agents don't clock out.

From goal to team

You state the objective. aiRA staffs it.

aiRA breaks a goal down into the customer groups and tasks it actually contains, then creates an agent for each — with its own job description, playbook, and limits. You review the whole team before anything runs.

Coverage

Every customer group watched. Every day. No exceptions.

Human teams have to prioritize, so the smaller customer groups go unworked. Agents don't triage — they cover. Every group gets an agent that checks its signals daily, acts when the moment is right, and asks a person when your rules require it. Nothing valuable slips through because nobody had time.

Lifecycle

Agents that manage themselves — inside your rules

Each agent runs a continuous five-stage loop, so the system improves without anyone babysitting it.

1

Slice

aiRA divides the goal into small, precise customer groups — each different enough to deserve its own approach.

2

Match

Each group is matched to the offer, message, and channel your data says fits it best.

3

Staff

A dedicated agent is created for each group — with clear limits, sent to you for approval.

4

Tune

Agents test variations, measure real results, and shift effort toward what's working.

5

Retire

When a group is served or an approach stops working, the agent winds itself down — and records why.
